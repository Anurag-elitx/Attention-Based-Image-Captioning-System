- **Mono label:** FAQ

### Q&A (accordion):
1. **What counts as a generation?**
   One generation = one photo uploaded + 8 captions produced. It doesn't matter which platforms or settings you pick — it's always one credit.

2. **Can I change my plan later?**
   Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from your dashboard. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle.

3. **Do unused credits roll over?**
   No. Credits reset at the start of each billing cycle. Free-tier credits don't expire.

4. **Is there a refund policy?**
   We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. No questions asked.

5. **What payment methods do you accept?**
   We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and PayPal through our payment processor, Stripe.
