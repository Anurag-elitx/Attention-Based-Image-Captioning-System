01
How does CapGen generate captions from my image?
+
CapGen uses a vision-language model to read your photo (subject, mood, composition, lighting) and then writes a caption for each network that matches its voice, length and hashtag conventions.
02
Which social networks are supported?
+
Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X (Twitter), Threads, WhatsApp, Snapchat, and LinkedIn. Each caption is tuned to the platform's native tone and character limits.
03
Does it work for any kind of photo?
+
Yes. Portraits, food, landscapes, products, events, pets. CapGen handles them all, and the tone and hashtag strategy adapts to the subject.
04
Is my data safe when I upload a photo?
+
Your photo is processed only to generate your captions, then stored in your account so it appears in your collections. Delete a generation and its image is removed; delete your account and everything goes. Nothing you upload is used to train AI models.
05
How good are the captions?
+
For most everyday photos, they land on the first try. You can regenerate any network's caption in one click, or set the tone and length before generating.
06
Can I steer the captions toward a tone or theme?
+
Yes. Set a tone, theme, or the tags you want in the controls, and CapGen leans into those cues while staying true to what is in the photo.
07
Can I add a personal signature or brand voice?
+
On any paid plan, yes. Save a signature or brand-voice note and CapGen applies it to every caption on your account.
