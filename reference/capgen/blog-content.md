## Blog Post 1: "How to write Instagram captions that actually get engagement"
- **URL:** /blog/instagram-captions-engagement
- **Category:** TIPS
- **Date:** November 15, 2026
- **Reading time:** 5 min read

### Body:
Let's skip the fluff. You've read "be authentic" and "tell a story" a thousand times. Here's what actually moves the needle on Instagram engagement in 2026.

**1. The first line is everything**
Instagram truncates captions after about 125 characters. If your hook doesn't land in that window, nobody taps "more." The best hooks create a gap between what the reader knows and what they want to know.

Examples that work:
- "I almost didn't post this."
- "Here's what nobody tells you about [topic]."
- "Stop scrolling — this matters."

**2. Write for the save, not the like**
The algorithm weighs saves more than likes. Captions that teach, list, or reveal something worth coming back to get saved. Think "5 tips" or "the exact process I used."

**3. Use line breaks aggressively**
Walls of text die on mobile. Break every 1-2 sentences. Use whitespace like a weapon.

**4. End with a question (but make it specific)**
"What do you think?" is lazy. "What's the one app you couldn't live without in 2026?" gets answers.

**5. Let CapGen do the heavy lifting**
Upload your photo to CapGen, pick your tone, and get 8 platform-tuned captions in seconds. Each one is already optimized for the platform you're posting to — including Instagram's ideal length and hashtag strategy.

## Blog Post 3: "The best time to post on every platform in 2026"
- **URL:** /blog/best-time-to-post-2026
- **Category:** SOCIAL MEDIA
- **Date:** October 28, 2026
- **Reading time:** 4 min read

### Body:
Every "best time to post" article gives you the same stale data from 2022. We looked at actual posting patterns from thousands of CapGen users to find what works right now.

**Instagram:** Tuesday and Wednesday, 10am–1pm local time. Reels do better on evenings (7-9pm).

**TikTok:** Tuesday, Thursday, Friday. Post between 2pm–5pm. The algorithm is less time-sensitive than Instagram.

**Facebook:** Wednesday and Thursday, 1pm–4pm. Longer captions perform better here than on other platforms.

**X (Twitter):** Weekdays, 8am–10am and 6pm–9pm. Short, punchy captions outperform threads for engagement.

**LinkedIn:** Tuesday through Thursday, 7am–8am and 5pm–6pm. The morning commute window is real.

**Threads:** Still early, but evenings (6pm–10pm) show the most activity. Casual, conversational captions win.

**WhatsApp Status:** Early morning (7am–9am) and late evening (8pm–11pm). Keep it short and visual.

**Snapchat:** Late afternoon (3pm–5pm) and evening (8pm–11pm). Younger audiences dominate.

**The bottom line**
These are starting points, not rules. Your audience might be different. Test, track, and adjust. And let CapGen handle the captions so you can focus on the creative.
